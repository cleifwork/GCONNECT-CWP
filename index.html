Voucher timer & pause

Know how your WiFi time works.

  • When time starts. Your timer starts after a successful voucher login. Checking your balance does not activate it.
  • Which plans can pause. Automatic pause/resume is available on eligible hourly plans of 12 hours or less.
  • How to pause. Turn off WiFi or disconnect from GCONNECT WIFI. A pause may take a moment while the network detects the disconnection.
  • How to resume. Reconnect using the same active voucher to continue using its remaining time.
  • Other plans. Plans without pause support keep counting down after activation, even while disconnected.
  • When time runs out. Internet access ends and the expired voucher can no longer be used.
